Week 2 · Regression

Regression is the entry point to supervised learning. We derive linear regression from first principles, implement gradient descent step by step, extend to polynomial features, and introduce regularisation as a principled defence against overfitting.

Intuition, mathematics, and implementation of the algorithm that trains virtually every ML model.

Lesson 2.3

Polynomial & Regularised Regression

Lesson 2.4

Evaluation Metrics

MSE, MAE, RMSE, R², and how to choose the right metric for your regression problem.
