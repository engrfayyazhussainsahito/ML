Week 03 Overview

Week 3 · Classification

We extend supervised learning to discrete outputs. Starting with logistic regression as the probabilistic generalisation of linear models, we add decision trees and KNN, and build a complete understanding of how to measure classification performance.

Lessons This Week

Lesson 3.1

Logistic Regression

The sigmoid function, log loss, decision boundaries, and multi-class softmax extension.

Lesson 3.2

Decision Trees

Gini impurity, information gain, recursive splitting, pruning, and depth control.

Lesson 3.3

K-Nearest Neighbours

Distance metrics, the curse of dimensionality, and choosing k.

Lesson 3.4

Classification Metrics

Accuracy, precision, recall, F1, ROC–AUC, and the confusion matrix in depth.
