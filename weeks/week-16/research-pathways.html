Lesson 16.4: Research Pathways

Is Research Right for You?

ML research involves generating novel knowledge — new algorithms, theoretical analyses, empirical insights, or new applications. It requires tolerance for long periods of uncertainty, the ability to read and situate your work in existing literature, and the ability to communicate clearly in writing. If you want to push the frontier rather than apply existing tools, a research pathway may suit you.

Building Research Credibility

early_steps = [
    "Reproduce a published result — learn the codebase, understand every detail",
    "Extend the reproduction: what happens if you change X?",
    "Write a blog post explaining the paper clearly (builds writing skills)",
    "Join a reading group: weekly paper discussions build breadth quickly",
    "Contribute to open-source ML projects (huggingface, pytorch-lightning)",
    "Submit to workshops before main conferences (lower bar, faster feedback)",
    "Collaborate: find a co-author via Twitter, Discord ML communities",
